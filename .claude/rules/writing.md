# Writing

No em-dashes and no en-dashes, anywhere. Not in the interface, not in comments, not in
commit messages, not in documentation. A comma, a full stop, or a rewritten sentence.

No marketing voice inside the application. No "Effortlessly", no "Seamlessly", no
"Supercharge", no exclamation marks. The marketing page is the only place with a
selling job.

Errors say what happened, then what to do, in that order.

> Could not reach the travel time service. Your stops are saved, try again in a moment.

Warnings name the specific conflict, with the actual numbers in it.

> Fish Market closes at 4:00 pm and you arrive at 4:30 pm.

Never "Timing issue detected". Never "Something went wrong". Never a warning that makes
the reader open something else to find out what it means.

Times are written as the reader would say them: 4:00 pm, 9:15 am, 12:00 pm. Durations
are written as "25 min", "1 hr 40 min". Never "0h25m", never decimal hours.

Labels are the words the user would use. "Time at places" rather than "Dwell time".
"Getting there" rather than "Transit leg".
